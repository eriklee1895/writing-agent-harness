## Fact boundary

- Current-event facts were rechecked on 2026-08-20 before promotion to `content/origin/`.
- The article distinguishes official product descriptions from the author's inference.
- DeepSeek Harness is described by its own source as a developer preview with expected compatibility-breaking changes.
- Anthropic's watermark article describes a detection signal, not proof of sole authorship or proof that text was written without human editing.
- OpenAI/Hugging Face and AISI materials describe evaluation/security incidents and attempted actions; the draft should separate confirmed actions, attempted actions, and interpretation.
- OpenAI's 2026-08-19 Zero Data Retention announcement is a company product and policy statement, not independent proof that all agent data handling is safe.
- A2A's interoperability scope should not be presented as a complete authorization, safety, or accountability solution.
